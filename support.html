DayFlow Support

DayFlow is a private offline planner for tasks, habits, important dates, notes, moods, and daily reflection.

Does DayFlow require an account?

No. DayFlow works without sign-in and stores content on your device. You do not need a DayFlow server account.

Does DayFlow upload my content?

No. DayFlow does not operate a content server or automatically upload your saved content. Copy places content on the system clipboard; Share lets you choose a destination. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and account settings.

How do I send feedback?

Open More > Contact & Feedback. Write your message, optionally add a reply email and basic app diagnostics, then choose Copy or Share. DayFlow never submits the message automatically.

You can also select Support email or email manhuzi09@gmail.com. The email link only opens your mail app; it does not send anything or automatically include the feedback editor's text. Copy and paste your feedback into your email, or share it to your mail app if available.

Why is voice input unavailable?

Voice input requires microphone and speech-recognition permissions and support for Apple's on-device speech recognition on your device and selected language. DayFlow does not fall back to online recognition. If it is unavailable, use keyboard input.

How do I back up my data?

Open More > Settings > Data & Sync and export JSON. CSV and calendar exports are also available under More data tools. Save exports somewhere you trust; DayFlow does not provide a server backup.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records, or use More > Settings > Data & Sync > More data tools > Delete all local data. Manage any exported copies and operating-system backups separately.
