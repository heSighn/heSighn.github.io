DayFlow Privacy Policy

DayFlow works without an account, advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

Tasks, events, habits, notes, moods, reviews, important dates, attachments, drafts, app preferences, and the optional privacy-lock setting are stored in the app container on your device. DayFlow does not automatically upload this content to the developer.

Permissions

Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested only when you start voice input. Speech recognition is required to run on the device and does not fall back to server recognition. If on-device recognition is unavailable, use keyboard input instead. Calendar access is requested only when you choose to add an item through the system calendar editor. Notification permission is requested only when you schedule a reminder. Photos and files are available only through system pickers after you select them.

Exports, feedback, and sharing

Copying content places it on the system clipboard. Export and feedback sharing occurs only when you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. The feedback composer does not submit anything automatically and has no developer-operated backend. Optional diagnostics are limited to the app version, build number, iOS version, device model, and selected app language; they never automatically include a device identifier or saved DayFlow content. The destination you choose may have its own privacy practices.

Support email

If you email manhuzi09@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and the message, attachments, or diagnostics you choose to send. This information is used to respond to and handle your support request, not for advertising or tracking. Please do not send sensitive personal content unless it is necessary for your request.

Support email is processed by Gmail under Google's privacy practices; it is not a DayFlow app backend. Opening the support email link launches your mail app and does not send a message or automatically attach your DayFlow content. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Backups

Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your device and account settings. DayFlow does not operate a separate cloud backup service.

Deletion

You can delete individual items or use More > Settings > Data & Sync > More data tools > Delete all local data. Removing the app also removes its local app container, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Copies you export, share, or retain in backups must be managed at those destinations.

This website

These static pages contain no analytics, tracking scripts, or contact forms. The hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs under its own privacy policy.

Children

DayFlow is a general productivity app and does not automatically collect personal data from children.
